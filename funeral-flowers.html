Funeral & Farewell Flowers

Having spent many years as a cancer nurse practitioner, I understand the profound importance of saying goodbye with dignity and love.

Creating funeral and farewell flowers has become one of the most meaningful aspects of my work. I approach each arrangement with the same care and compassion I brought to my nursing practice, knowing that these flowers will help families honour their loved ones during their most difficult moments.

Whether you're looking for traditional funeral tributes or contemporary celebration-of-life arrangements, I create each piece with deep respect for the person being remembered and the family left behind.

My experience supporting families through loss has taught me that every goodbye is unique. I listen carefully to understand what would feel right for your loved one.

I grow my flowers without harsh chemicals, and source locally where possible. There's something deeply meaningful about using flowers that grew in the same soil, under the same Devon skies, as the person we're remembering.

I can deliver to funeral homes, churches, and crematoriums throughout East Devon, including Honiton, Cullompton, Ottery St Mary, Sidmouth, and the surrounding villages. I also deliver to the beautiful site at Hackpen Natural Burial Ground

"Every arrangement is created with the same care I would want for my own family."

What to Expect

When you contact me about funeral flowers, I'll listen carefully to understand what would feel right for your loved one. There's no pressure - just gentle guidance if you'd like it.

I can work with your funeral director, or you can contact me directly. I'm experienced in coordinating with local funeral directors in East Devon and understand their requirements and timings.
