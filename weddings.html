Wedding venue flowers

I love meeting couples to fully appreciate what look you'd like to create, and what would work well for your wedding venue. I offer initial telephone consultations and venue visits, to ensure your vision is brought to life.

Every wedding is unique, and I can provide flowers that work for different venues, styles and budgets. I love working with brides and grooms who have a strong clear vision for their wedding flowers, as much as I relish working with couples who need plenty of support and guidance.

The beauty of locally grown flowers is capturing the seasonality of your big day. If however, you have a particular flower that you love adore, I will endeavour to source it for you to work within my arrangement. It is very important to me to consider the environmental impact of floristry, and I therefore hugely advocate using British grown flowers only, avoiding any air miles which have a huge impact on global warming. British flowers frequently offer a fabulous scent which imported flowers never can. I often have bridal parties commenting on the beautiful scent from their bouquets.

I can make small table arrangements, large urns, decorative arches, bud vases and stunning bridal bouquets to set your wedding apart. I have a wide selection of beautiful vases and vessels that ensure your flowers offer individual style.

If you would prefer to arrange the flowers yourself I can be on hand, with buckets and snips and help you pick your own flowers. I will be able to help guide you with colours and flowers which complement each other and offer advice on how best to condition your flowers.

You can combine DIY flowers with some of the key elements, such as a bridal bouquet and button holes.

Your flowers will be ready the day before for collection, or I can deliver on the morning of your wedding, allowing you to focus on enjoying every moment.

Having a winter wedding? I can make dried flower arrangements that allow you to enjoy them for months to come.
