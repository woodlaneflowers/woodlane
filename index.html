I create bespoke flower arrangements which are harmonious with the seasons and provide a symbiotic relationship with wildlife and insects. I grow all my flowers on my flower farm in Broadhembury in East Devon.

The imperfection of a wonky stem or petal is attractive to me because it fits perfectly with my wild, romantic arranging style. I bring my arrangements together with hedgerow foliage to add texture and movement.

My peak season sees me delivering subscription flowers which keep businesses and homes looking fabulous. I also create wedding flowers, event flowers, one off bouquets as gifts, small but perfectly formed posies in a recycled tin and funeral flowers. I also harvest some of the flowers to create unique dried arrangements and wreaths during the cooler months. As I enter into a new year, it's time to be sowing and propagating again. It's the constant evolution of seasons that inspires my creative flair.

I can deliver locally to locations like Honiton, Payhembury, Kentisbeare, Cullompton, Ottery St. Mary and Sidmouth on arrangement.

Meet Sally & Our Flower Farm

Hello, I'm Sally. My lifelong passion for gardens and growing beautiful, natural flowers led me to create Wood Lane Flowers. Nestled in Broadhembury, East Devon, our flower farm is where I cultivate a diverse array of seasonal blooms, inspired by the stunning local landscape.

I believe in the charm of British-grown flowers and the personal touch they bring to every occasion.
