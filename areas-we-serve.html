Proudly serving our East Devon community

Living in the heart of East Devon in Broadhembury, I feel incredibly fortunate to be surrounded by such beautiful countryside and wonderful communities.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of creating flowers for celebrations and farewells throughout this special corner of Devon. Each village and town has its own character, and I love being part of the moments that matter most to families here.

I regularly deliver possies and bouquets to Joshua's Harvest Store in Ottery St Mary and Otter Valley Dairy in Monkton where I have fresh, seasonal flowers available to buy, restocking on Fridays. I also also deliver flowers to couples on their wedding day and to families saying goodbye to loved ones. There's something deeply satisfying about knowing these locally grown flowers are marking the most important occasions in our community's life.

I deliver throughout East Devon, typically within a 20-mile radius of Broadhembury. The beauty of growing flowers locally means I can offer same-day delivery for urgent occasions, particularly for funeral arrangements when families need support quickly.

Every delivery is personal to me - whether it's arriving early on a wedding morning to dress a venue, or quietly placing a sympathy arrangement when a family is grieving. These aren't just transactions; they're moments where flowers can bring comfort, joy, or simply show that someone cares.

If you're not in one of the areas listed below, please still get in touch. I often make special arrangements for occasions that are particularly meaningful, and I'm always happy to chat about what might be possible.

Special Places I've Had the Joy to Dress

Broadhembury Church

There's something magical about creating flowers for my own village church. The ancient stones and beautiful stained glass windows provide the perfect backdrop for romantic, wild arrangements that feel at home in this sacred space.

Deer Park Country House, Buckerell

This stunning venue with its elegant rooms and beautiful grounds allows me to create arrangements that are both sophisticated and naturally romantic. The house and gardens complement my style perfectly.

The Corn Barn, Cullompton

A barn full of character, charm and natural beauty. It has rustic chic ambience, giving a relaxed, warm homely atmosphere. A family run business and feel that has the personal touch.

Glebe House, Southleigh

This beautiful Georgian house provides an elegant setting where seasonal British flowers can really shine. The period features and beautiful proportions call for arrangements that are both refined and naturally beautiful.

Each venue has its own personality, and I love the challenge of creating flowers that enhance the natural beauty of these special East Devon locations.
